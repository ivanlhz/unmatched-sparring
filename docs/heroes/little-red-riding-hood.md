# Little Red Riding Hood (Little Red vs. Beowulf, season 1)

Playable by the bot and by the human, either side, against every other hero. Data read from the real card images (sheet 177 of the tournament savegame, the hero card 310 on sheet 3) on 2026-11-22. Rulings: Ivan 2026-11-22 (`RULINGS-batch4`: LR1) and the readings of `REGLAS-PENDIENTES-batch4` section 2.

## Summary
- Melee hero, 14 life, move 2, with one sidekick, the **Huntsman** (ranged, 9 life, move 2). Hero fid `red`, sidekick fid `huntsman`, slug `little-red-riding-hood`.
- Ability: "Resolve an effect on a card you play if the symbol next to the effect matches the item in your basket. At the start of the game, place LITTLE RED'S BASKET in your discard pile. The star counts as any one wolf, rose or axe symbol." The basket is the top card of her discard pile; the Huntsman's cards use it too ("a card you play").
- Deck: 30 cards, `CardID = 17700 + idx` (sheet 177; the deck object holds 31 cards: the 30 and the character card 310). Order on the sheet: What Large Hands You Have 0-1, What Big Ears You Have 2-3, Long Have I Sought You 4-6, What Big Eyes You Have 7-8, What's That In My Basket? 9-12, Once Upon a Time 13-14, Stones in the Belly 15-17, What a Terrible Big Mouth You Have 18-19, The Wolf's Skin 20-22, Never Leave the Path 23-24, Into the Woods 25-27, A Grimm Tale 28-29. Bands (card backs): LITTLE RED (Large Hands, Big Ears, Big Eyes, Basket, Terrible Big Mouth, Never Leave the Path, Into the Woods), HUNTSMAN (Long Have I Sought You, A Grimm Tale), ANY (Once Upon a Time, Stones in the Belly, The Wolf's Skin).
- Table: bag `9a5f4e` (inner `7d8bbe`), miniature `eb1971`, life dial `ca8e35` (max 14, shared with Donatello, Chupacabra and Tomoe Gozen: told apart by name), the Huntsman token `83d1f2` with his dial `091f30` (max 9), deck `75e561`, and the loose Basket card `51f3b8`.

## The basket and the ORDER of the discard pile
The engine's discard pile is a list: `Game.discard_card` appends, so the newest card is `discard[-1]`. The basket is the symbol of that card. This is the first hero whose effects depend on the order of a pile, so the order is now part of the state: it is saved (`persist` writes the ids in order and `apply_game` reads them back in order), cloned for the search, and part of the search's cache key (`EngineState._pure_loss_key` keys on `ids(discard)` in order).

- **The starting Basket is not a card.** It is never drawn, counted or returned. With no card in the pile it is what lies there (`basket()` answers `wild`); the thirty cards are all in the deck, the hand and the pile as usual. On the table it IS an object, a loose card in her discard zone (see Table).
- **The symbol of a card** is the icon in the lower left corner of the image, kept as the tag `symbol:wolf|rose|axe|wild` on each copy (`wild` is the star of What's That In My Basket?). Findings on the images: the three copies of **Into the Woods** print ONE symbol each (rose, wolf, axe: idx 25, 26, 27), not three at once, so Ivan's LR1 question about Into the Woods does not arise: only the starting Basket and the star card are wild. The copies are three different cards (different tags).
- **Read when each line resolves.** A line with a symbol is `{"test": "plugin", "id": "red:basket", "key": "wolf"}` inside an `if`; a line with no symbol is not guarded. The combat cards reach the pile last (`CombatFlow` discards them at the end), a BOOST card is on the pile as soon as it is boosted, and a card the opponent makes her discard (Ambush) lands on her pile at once, so the basket CAN change in the middle of a combat (`test_a_card_discarded_in_the_middle_of_the_combat_changes_the_basket_for_the_lines_that_follow`).
- **A wild basket is ONE symbol its player chooses** (Ivan 2026-11-22, LR1): the starting Basket and a star card on top. The question is `ChoiceRequest("effect")` with `tag: "basket"` and three options (wolf, rose, axe). It is asked the first time a line with a symbol needs it and kept in that run's flags, so Never Leave the Path (three lines) asks once. Design note: Ivan's text says "chosen when the card is played"; the engine asks at the first line that needs it, which is the same moment for every card of the deck (all of them resolve their symbol lines after the card is played and none changes the basket before that line) and needs no new "when a card is played" hook. Pending Ivan 1.
- **Audit of every place that appends to or takes from a pile** (`tests/test_little_red_pile_order.py` keeps it true): `Game.discard_card`, `ops_core` discards, `ops_combat` boost discards, `ongoing`, `inventions`, `plugins_strange`, `tricks` and `combat_flow.replace_attack_card` only append; `ops_info.put_in_hand`/`return_to_deck`, `table.py`, `session_effects`, `Game.remove_from_game` and the session's lifts remove by identity, which leaves the order of the others alone. Whole-pile assignments are only `persist.apply_game` (in saved order) and the adoption of the bot's table (`session.adopt_table`: the engine's cards that are on the table keep their order, unknown ones are added after them). Loki's Sindri's Bet and Devil of Hell's Kitchen read the newest card with `pick: last`; Pandora's Hindsight and Achilles name a card. No effect of any hero reads `top` of a pile (the data test rejects it).
- **The human's pile.** The engine keeps the order of everything it does itself (the cards of a combat, a BOOST, an effect). Cards the human moves onto the pile BY HAND that the bot only sees on the table (a hand-limit discard, a scheme) land in the order of their CardIDs when several arrive in one step: the table does not tell the bot which of them was put down last. A single card, the usual case, is exact. The chat names the basket at every line that depends on it ("cesta: rosa"), so the human can see if it disagrees with their pile. Pending Ivan 2.

## Cards (as implemented)
Symbols in the text are written (wolf), (rose), (axe); the star is "(The star counts as any one wolf, rose or axe symbol.)".
1. **What Large Hands You Have** (attack 2 / 3, Little Red, corner wolf): AFTER "You may return this card to your hand. (rose) Instead, you may return the top card of your discard pile to your hand." The first line has no symbol: always. With the basket on a rose the second line REPLACES it: the newest card of the pile (`pick: last`) returns instead of this card; with nothing to return (the Basket chosen as a rose is not a card) nothing happens and this card does not return either (Discord). Both are optional. This card is not in the pile yet, so "the top card" is what lay there before, a BOOST card of the combat included. The human's question names the card ("devuelves Once Upon a Time, la carta de arriba de tu descarte...").
2. **What Big Ears You Have** (attack 4 / 2, Little Red, corner wolf): "(axe) You may play this card as a defense card." A property of the card, not an op: `Game.check_defense_card` accepts it as a defense while the basket is an axe or wild (`plugins_little_red.may_defend_with`, tag `defend_if_axe`), checked when the card is played; once played as a defense it stays one whatever cancels it. The bot's choice of a defense never offers it (`can_defend` is false for an attack): the bot never defends with Big Ears; the human may. Pending Ivan 3.
3. **Long Have I Sought You** (attack 4 / 2, Huntsman, corner axe): DURING, if the Huntsman is adjacent to the opposing fighter the value is 6 instead (`set_value 6`, bonuses stay). No symbol.
4. **What Big Eyes You Have** (versatile 3 / 2, Little Red, corner wolf): IMMEDIATELY cancel all effects on the opponent's card; (rose) also ignore its value (`set_value ignore`). unmatched-memory says value 2: a slip, the image says 3.
5. **What's That In My Basket?** (versatile 4 / 3, Little Red, corner star): no effect; wild on top of the pile.
6. **Once Upon a Time** (versatile 2 / 1, any, corner rose): AFTER (axe) 3 effect damage to the opposing fighter.
7. **Stones in the Belly** (versatile 2 / 1, any, corner rose): DURING (axe) the opponent discards 1 random card and its BOOST is added to this card's value (plugin `red:stones`, the Ambush flow: for the human the bot names a position and reads the card). unmatched-memory has no value (and Ivan's note said none): the image prints 2.
8. **What a Terrible Big Mouth You Have** (defense 2 / 2, Little Red, corner axe): AFTER (wolf) damage to the opposing fighter equal to the printed value of their card.
9. **The Wolf's Skin** (defense 2 / 2, any, corner axe): AFTER (wolf) draw 2 cards.
10. **Never Leave the Path** (scheme, boost 1, Little Red, corner wolf): (wolf) place Little Red in any space and gain 1 action; (axe) 2 damage to each opposing fighter in her zone (read after the wolf line); (rose) draw 3 cards.
11. **Into the Woods** (scheme, boost 2, Little Red, corners rose / wolf / axe): move Little Red up to 3 spaces; gain 1 action. No symbol.
12. **A Grimm Tale** (scheme, boost 3, Huntsman, corner rose): Little Red recovers 2 health; (wolf) 4 instead. The Huntsman plays it; the basket is the player's.

## Discrepancies with unmatched-memory
- What Big Eyes You Have: versatile, value 3 (memory 2). Stones in the Belly: value 2 (memory none). Both from the images.
- Titles carry the corner symbols as emoji in memory ("Never Leave the Path 🐺"); the overlay's `memory_title` keeps them, the HeroDef spells the title as printed. Into the Woods' three copies are one title with three symbols in memory.
- The symbols of the effect lines (the icon in front of each line) are in memory's text as emoji; the HeroDef text writes them as (wolf), (rose), (axe).
- Everything else (copies, 30 cards, 14 life, move 2, the Huntsman's 9) agrees with the images.

## Table (what the bot does, what the chat asks, what the bot reads back)
- **The Basket is an object.** The loose card `51f3b8` of her bag (CardID 17600 of a one-image sheet: the number of the sheet-176 decks' first card, but another image and nobody's card) is a `token` named `basket`, so the pre-game gate lays it down with her other objects. `token_slots.basket` puts it inside her discard zone: on the bot's side at the first slot of the discard grid (the bot's first discard lands on it, so it is the bottom of the pile), on the human's side in the middle of their zone. Both spots are estimates (verify live). `tools/import_save.py` accepts a loose card named in `token_names`.
- **No read takes it for a card.** Every read of a discard pile is cut down to the side's own CardIDs before the engine sees it (`_table_discard`, `_land_in_discard`, the human reads), and her range is 17700-17729, so the Basket (17600) is nobody's. The matrix lays it in her pile in every game and checks no stray-card warning appears.
- **Bot as Little Red.** The engine chooses the wild symbol with its own policy (`ai_little_red`); the chat line of each card says what the basket was ("cesta: lobo", "cesta comodin contada como hacha"); the discard grid shows the pile in order of play. The bot reads nothing of the human's hand: Stones in the Belly makes the human discard the position the bot names (the Ambush flow).
- **Human as Little Red.** A wild basket is a question on the panel with three buttons ("lobo / rosa / hacha"), asked when the first line with a symbol resolves; a basket that is not wild is not asked, and the chat says what it was. What Large Hands You Have asks "devuelves X, la carta de arriba de tu descarte..." and the bot lifts the card from their pile into their hand by its id. What Big Ears You Have face down as a defense is accepted at the Reveal when the basket allows it, and refused with a message that says why when it does not.
- **Save and resume** in the middle of the wild-basket question: the question and its three options are saved with the run (`test_the_question_survives_a_save_and_is_answered_after_the_resume`).

## AI (`sparring/ai_little_red.py`)
- A wild basket: the symbol of the card's only symbol line; What Large Hands You Have is a rose only when the pile has a card to bring back; Never Leave the Path weighs 2 damage to each rival in her zone (a kill counts double), 3 cards (as many as fit in the hand and the deck) and a free place with an action (1.4). The rollout chooser (`SyntheticChooser`) gives the same answer. The first estimates have not been tuned against games.
- What the search sees: the pile order is in the cache key; the starting Basket is a wild symbol; the card the opponent may hold is not read (Stones makes the rival discard at random).
- Known limits: the bot never defends with Big Ears (its choice of defenses is by card type); it does not plan the order of its discards to set up a basket.
