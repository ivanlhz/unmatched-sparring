## ESPIONAGE as implemented
- **Counters** (`ability.counters` of the effects file): `ruse_tokens` (the supply, 0..4, starts at 4) and `ruse` (0..1: 1 while a ruse token lies next to the card of the attack in progress). The bot's `ruse` is a physical token: a copy of the Ruse stack laid at the bot's token spot when it goes on and removed when the combat ends (the engine's generic bot-token machinery, `session_translators._counter_bot`); the supply has no object of its own, the stack stays where it is and the chat says how many are left. The human's tokens are theirs: the chat says "place one next to the card", "take it off" and how many are left.
- **Trigger `espionage`** (event `attacked` with `target: {side: enemy}`, i.e. one of your fighters attacks; condition: a token in the supply and none on a card yet): plugin `washington:use_ruse` asks the attacker "a ruse token on this attack?" **only for a card that carries a ruse effect** (tag `ruse`: a token on any other card changes nothing for the attacker). Yes: `ruse_tokens` -1, `ruse` 1. Any fighter of Washington's may do it (a Spy attacking with Sabotage or Circumvent too).
- **Trigger `espionage_remove`** (same event, condition: a token on the card): plugin `washington:offer_removal` asks the OPPONENT "discard 1 random card to remove the ruse token?" before they choose their defense (the trigger runs before `choose_defense`, in `Game._declare_attack`, in the search, and at the table before the bot's defense is placed or before the human may reveal). Yes: a random card of their hand is discarded (a voluntary discard: `discard` with `voluntary`, the table's random-discard route for the human) and `ruse` goes back to 0. The token is gone: it is not returned to the supply (Pending Ivan 3). With an empty hand there is no question.
- **Trigger `espionage_clear`** (`after_attack`): `ruse` 0 when the combat is over.
- **The [RUSE] part of a card** is a plain condition `{"test": "counter", "name": "ruse", "min": 1}` inside the moment: Agent 711 (AFTER: if you won, 7 damage to the opposing fighter), Circumvent (DURING: ignore the value of the opponent's card), Sabotage (AFTER: if you won, the opponent discards 2 random cards) and Allies Everywhere (AFTER: Washington recovers 3 health). It is the reading "applies only if the ruse token is still on the card" (Pending Ivan 1). A Feint (or any cancel) on the card cancels the ruse effect like any other effect of it; the token is spent all the same.
- **The human is Washington** (`session_espionage.py`): the attack card lies face down, so at Reveal, before the bot chooses its defense, the panel asks Yes / No (the slot Perfect Shot's prediction uses, `Pending.predict`, answered by `hidden.answer_prediction`, which presses Reveal again). The answer is kept in `Pending.prediction["ruse"]`, given to the `attacked` event through `RuseChooser`, and the bot answers its own question with its AI: if it discards a card the chat says so, the card reaches its pile on the table in the same step, and only then does it choose its defense. A card with no ruse effect, no token left, an attack already declared (a cancelled attack carried over) or a hero that is not Washington asks nothing. Saved and resumed like Perfect Shot's question.
- **The bot is Washington**: its attack card is placed face down; `bot_attack_placed` raises `attacked` (the bot's AI decides the token, the copy is laid on the table, the chat says it) and, when the human has cards, the trigger parks the question "discard 1 random card to remove the ruse token?" on the panel (a parked run like FATEFUL BARGAINS). Until it is answered no other button works. Yes: the discard is the usual random one ("la carta N de tu mano contando desde la izquierda", settled with End action), the token goes and the card's ruse effect is lost. No: the card keeps it. When the combat ends the copy is taken off the table (`declared["counters"]` is the baseline of the end-of-combat diff).

## Cards (as implemented)
1. **Lead Astray** (attack 5 / boost 2, Washington): AFTER, move a fighter in the combat up to 2 spaces (`move` of `combatant`, the owner chooses who and where), won or lost. Skirmish's move without the condition.
2. **Agent 711** (attack 4 / 3, Washington): AFTER, [RUSE] if you won, 7 effect damage to the opposing fighter.
3. **Allies Everywhere** (attack 1 / 3, Washington): AFTER, gain 1 free action to attack (`gain_action` with `only: attack`: Maneuvers and schemes cannot spend it; Decided by Ivan 1); [RUSE] Washington recovers 3 health (the hero, whoever played the card).
4. **Circumvent** (attack 3 / 2, ANY): DURING, [RUSE] ignore the value of the opponent's card (`set_value ignore`).
5. **Sabotage** (attack 3 / 3, ANY): AFTER, [RUSE] if you won, the opponent discards 2 random cards (`pick: random`: the bot reads nothing; the human's are theirs by position).
6. **Misinformation** (versatile 4 / 2, ANY): AFTER, if you won the combat, regain a ruse token (`ruse_tokens` +1, never above 4; in defense it works when the defender wins).
7. **Make A Stand** (versatile 3 / 1, ANY): DURING, if the fighter started this turn in its current space its value becomes 5 (plugin test `washington:started_here`: the space recorded at the start of the turn equals the current one; it may leave and come back; a Spy placed this turn has no recorded space and does not count).
8. **Feint** (versatile 2 / 3, ANY): the shared Feint (source id 2): cancel all effects on the opponent's card.
9. **Recruit To The Ring** (versatile 2 / 1, ANY): AFTER, return a defeated Spy next to Washington (1 life); if you won, to any space instead (`revive`; the human places the miniature by hand, the chat says "una casilla adyacente" or "cualquier casilla").
10. **Network Of Spies** (versatile 2 / 2, Washington): DURING +1 for each other friendly fighter in Washington's zone.
11. **Undercover Agent** (defense 1 / 2, Washington): DURING, Washington may swap spaces with an adjacent Spy; if he does the Spy is the defender (`swap` + plugin `washington:replace_combatant`, Houdini's mechanism: the Spy takes the blow, which with 1 life means it falls).
12. **Sympathetic Stain** (scheme, boost 3, band SPY): played by a Spy. Look at the opponent's hand (always); if a Spy is adjacent to one of their fighters (`exists` with `filter.adjacent_to_any`), choose a card of their hand for them to discard and regain a ruse token. The bot discards the most valuable card it saw; the human pings the card as in every look-and-discard.
13. **Gather Information** (scheme, boost 2, Washington): draw 3, move each of your fighters up to 3 spaces, through opposing fighters (Close For The Kill's move).

## Decided by Ivan (2026-11-13)
1. **"Gain 1 free action to attack"** is attack-only: `gain_action` with `only: attack` (the engine's `fx.attack_credit`); a Maneuver or a scheme cannot spend it, the bot or the human (`tests/test_george_washington_effects.py`).
2. **Make A Stand**: "started this turn in their current space" compares the current space with the one recorded at the start of the turn; the fighter may leave and come back and it still counts; a fighter with no recorded space (a Spy placed this turn) does not.
3. **[RUSE] means "applies only if the ruse token is still on the card".**
4. **Any fighter of Washington's, a Spy included, may use a ruse token** when it attacks.

## Pending Ivan (rules taken as the most literal reading)
1. **A token the opponent removes is gone**: it is spent and does not return to the supply (the card says "remove the ruse token"; "regain a ruse token" is a separate effect). Ivan does not know; kept as implemented. Alternative: it goes back to the supply.
2. **A token is offered only on a card with a ruse effect.** On any other card it would do nothing for the attacker and only cost the opponent a decision (and a card).
3. **The human declares the token at Reveal**, after the card is face down and before the bot defends; a cancelled attack carries the declaration over (Ivan's Abe ruling, 29/10), so the same attack is not asked again.
4. **Recruit To The Ring** brings the Spy back with 1 life; with no free space next to Washington the lost-combat case finds nowhere to put it and nothing happens.
5. **Undercover Agent** is optional ("may"); with several adjacent Spies the owner chooses. The Spy takes the combat damage and the card's value; the attacker's card stays aimed at the same space.
6. **Sympathetic Stain**: the adjacency of a Spy to a fighter of theirs is read when the card resolves (after the look); the token it gives back is clipped at 4.
7. **Sabotage and Agent 711** need the combat won; Agent 711's damage is effect damage (a status that stops effect damage stops it).

## AI
- The AI profile comes from the data (Washington `hero`, melee; the Spies `keep_away`, 3 of them: 1 life, so they are the shield and the price of Undercover Agent).
- **Values that move** (`ai_washington`, hooked next to Sinbad's in `ai_medusa`): Make A Stand is a 5 when its fighter has not moved (`own_value`, attacking and defending), Network Of Spies 2 plus the friends in his zone, Circumvent ignores the defense while a token can go on it (`circumvents`), and the AFTER of the others is priced (`after_value`: Agent 711's 7 against the target's hit points and the chance of winning, Sabotage's two cards, Allies Everywhere's heal and action, the token Misinformation gives back, the Spy Recruit To The Ring returns), the ruse parts weighted by the chance the token stays (`1 - P_REMOVE`, an assumption of 0.3).
- **Spending a token** (`ai_washington.use_ruse`, answered by `EffectAI._optional` for the request the plugin builds, so the search bot too): the effect's worth (with the chance the opponent discards a card instead, which is a card lost for them) against what a token is worth to keep (0.3, plus 0.15 per token already spent). In practice Agent 711, Circumvent and Sabotage get one nearly always, Allies Everywhere only when Washington has hit points to heal, and the last token is kept for a card that is worth it.
- **Removing the human's token** (`ai_washington.remove_ruse`): public information only. The card played is never read (the request carries the attacker and the target, not the card); the bot weighs the unseen ruse cards of Washington's deck (his full set minus his public discard pile) against a random card of its own hand: Agent 711 only against its hero and only for the 7 on top of an assumed 2.5 of combat damage (a kill counts), Circumvent for the defense it makes worthless, Sabotage for two cards.
- The search (`make_bot_ai({"ai": "ismcts"})`, the bot that plays live): its rollouts answer with `SyntheticChooser` (the attacker spends a token, the opponent keeps its card: `washington` extra), the plan is the same tree as every hero's and the live answers come from the heuristic `EffectAI` (`IsmctsMedusa.choose_effect`). Verified with the search on both seats (`tests/test_george_washington_ai.py`, the matrix).
